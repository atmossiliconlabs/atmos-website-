06 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Can individual students enrol?

Our programs currently run through partner colleges. If you're a student, ask your department to contact us, or send an enquiry and we'll let you know when open batches launch.

Do students need prior VLSI experience?

The workshop has no prerequisites. The skill development program assumes basic digital electronics, and we review the fundamentals before any tool work begins.

Which tools are used? Are licences needed?

Icarus Verilog, GTKWave, Yosys, OpenSTA, OpenTimer, OpenROAD and OpenLane, with Magic, KLayout and Netgen for physical verification, all on Linux. All are open source, so no licences are required.

What infrastructure does a college need?

A computer per student is preferred, running Ubuntu Linux with 16 GB RAM, a modern multi-core processor and 50–100 GB of free storage. For online sessions, students need a stable connection and a headset with a microphone.

Do students receive a certificate?

Yes. Certificates are issued by Atmos Silicon Labs Pvt. Ltd. and can be co-branded with your institution.
